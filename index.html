Summary of the Statement of Purpose

It is the purpose of the Glen’s Residential Home to ensure the Residents in our home receive the highest standard of care, respect and dignity at all times. We believe all our Resident’s should be cared for as we would like our own families and ourselves to be cared for. Care in the Glen’s includes assessing and meeting individual social, spiritual, emotional, physical and medical needs; while providing the appropriate number of trained staff to do so.

Philosophy of Care

The Glens Residential Home accommodates and offers Residential care for 16 people. Previously a family run home who had more than 25 years’ experience working in the Nursing and Residential Home business. The new owner Muhammed Majeed Consultant Diabetes and Endocrine will not be making any changes to the Glens Residential home, as he shares the same values and principles as the previous owners. As a provider of residential Care for older people we believe a homely and happy atmosphere is essential for our Residents.

We acknowledge the principles of “Homes are for living” with particular emphasis on: Privacy, Dignity, Respect, Independence, Choice, Rights and Fulfilment. Our ethos challenges us to provide care and support that meets and exceeds the minimum standards and regulations required by local Government.

Why Choose Glens Residential Home?

We are a family-run home with 16 single rooms in a single-story purpose-built facility, refurbished in 2015. Our focus is on personalised care, familiar faces, and a warm, homely atmosphere.
