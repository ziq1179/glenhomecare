A Place to Call Home in the Heart of the Glens

Nestled in the scenic beauty of the Glens — surrounded by mountains, valleys, and the breathtaking Antrim coast — our 16-bed, purpose-built home offers a safe and tranquil environment. We pride ourselves on being a community where life is defined by dignity and a true sense of belonging.

Our Purpose

To provide a nurturing, safe environment where residents receive person-centred care that promotes dignity and independence within our scenic coastal setting.

Care from the Heart

Our ”Care from the Heart” approach ensures every resident is treated as family, delivering compassionate support tailored to individual physical and emotional needs.

We are guided by the principles of Privacy, Dignity, Respect, Independence, Choice, Rights and Fulfilment — going beyond minimum standards to deliver the kind of care we would want for our own loved ones.

Why Choose Glens Residential Home?

We are a family-run home with 16 single rooms in a single-story purpose-built facility, refurbished in 2015. Our focus is on personalised care, familiar faces, and a warm, homely atmosphere.

Our Services

We provide person-centred residential care for older adults, people living with dementia, and adults with disabilities. Every resident receives tailored support in a warm, homely setting.

Book a Visit

Elderly Care

Our elderly care focuses on dignity, independence, and wellbeing. With 16 single rooms in a single-story, purpose-built home, we offer a safe and comfortable environment. We work closely with families, GPs, and district nurses to ensure each resident’s health and preferences are at the centre of their care.

Dementia Care

We support residents living with dementia with understanding and consistency. Our team is trained to create a calm, familiar routine, and our family-run approach means residents see the same faces every day. We aim to reduce anxiety and support quality of life through structure, activities, and a caring environment.

Disability Care

We provide tailored support for adults with physical or learning disabilities. Our accessible, single-story layout and dedicated staff help residents live as independently as possible while having support on hand when needed. Care plans are developed with the resident and their family or advocates.

Activities and visitors

Care with us includes a full rhythm of life: regular visitors from the community and daily activities led or joined by our staff—so residents stay stimulated, social, and supported.

Our community

Dining

Our kitchen is led by our Head Cook, who focuses on traditional, home-cooked Northern Irish favourites. Meals are prepared with care and we cater to dietary needs and preferences. Dining is a social time, with residents encouraged to enjoy meals together in a relaxed setting.

Community

We believe in staying part of the wider community. We welcome weekly visits from local nursery children and support trips to Cushendall village so residents can stay connected to the area they know and love.

Meet the Team

Glens Residential Home is family-run with a strong focus on continuity of care. Our residents see familiar faces every day, and our team is led by a hands-on Registered Manager.

Book a Visit

Leadership

Siobhan McHugh is our Registered Manager and is hands-on in the running of the home. She works closely with residents, families, and staff to ensure high standards of care and a welcoming, organised environment. You can expect to see her around the home and at the heart of day-to-day care.

Our Staff Philosophy

We are a family-run home (Glens Properties LLP, McKillop Family) and we place a strong emphasis on staff retention. That means residents and families get to know the same team over time, building trust and consistency. We believe familiar faces and a stable team are essential to quality care.

The Kitchen

Our Head Cook leads the kitchen with a focus on traditional, home-cooked Northern Irish favourites. Meals are prepared with care and we cater to dietary requirements. Good food and a homely dining experience are central to life at the Glens.
