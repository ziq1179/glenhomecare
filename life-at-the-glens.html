Life at the Glens

Every day at Glens Residential Home is built around comfort, activity, and community. From home-cooked meals to trips into Cushendall, we aim to make life here feel like home.

Activities and visitors

Life at the Glens is enriched by regular visitors from the community and by our staff, who take part in activities with residents every day—so there is always something to look forward to.

People who visit the home

  • Whiskey Lullaby’s — musicians who come every fortnight.
  • Anne Marie Mc Kendry — visits once a month for music.
  • Activities with Nuala — once a week: arts and crafts, games, and more.
  • Fiona Jones — chair yoga once a month.

Daily activities with our staff

Our team join in with residents every day for bingo, Play Your Cards Right, ball games, jigsaws, arts and crafts, and chair exercises—alongside quiet time in the lounge or garden when people prefer to relax.

Our community

Dining

Our kitchen is led by our Head Cook, who focuses on traditional, home-cooked Northern Irish favourites. Meals are prepared with care and we cater to dietary needs and preferences. Dining is a social time, with residents encouraged to enjoy meals together in a relaxed setting.

Community

We believe in staying part of the wider community. We welcome weekly visits from local nursery children and support trips to Cushendall village so residents can stay connected to the area they know and love.
