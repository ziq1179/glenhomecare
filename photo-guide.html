Photo guide for the website

Use this checklist when taking or choosing photos for the Glens Residential Home website. Each item corresponds to a slot you can manage in the admin area.

Video: In admin, you can paste a YouTube or Vimeo page URL, or a direct link to an MP4/WebM file (hosted on your site or elsewhere), into any slot field instead of a photo—the site will show a player in that place.

Exterior & grounds

  • A welcoming shot of the front entrance. Slot: exterior_entrance
  • The landscaped garden and outdoor seating areas. Slot: garden_grounds
  • A scenic shot showing the surrounding "Glens" landscape. Slot: glens_landscape

Interior living spaces

  • A brightly lit, clean single bedroom (ideally one personalized with a resident’s own furniture). Slot: bedroom
  • The main communal lounge / sitting area. Slot: lounge
  • The dining room set for a meal. Slot: dining

Life at the Glens

  • A "plated" shot of a home-cooked meal or afternoon tea. Slot: meal_plated
  • A photo of a quiet activity (e.g. a reading nook or board games). Slot: activity
  • If consent is in place: A photo of the local nursery children during their weekly visit. Slot: nursery_visit

Our team

  • A professional, friendly headshot of the Registered Manager. Slot: manager_headshot
  • A group photo of the care and kitchen staff in uniform. Slot: staff_group

Life at the Glens (many photos)

These slots power the Life at the Glens page. In Admin, each “Life gallery” and “Life page” entry matches a photo in order on that page. Upload or paste a URL for each. One slot, life_hero_lounge, updates both the top right image and the matching balloon / intergenerational picture in the long gallery, so you only change that picture once.

Slots: life_hero_arts, life_hero_lounge, and life_gal_01 through life_gal_26. services_elderly_care is the bedroom photo on Our Services.

Upload your photos in Admin (saves to the server) or to another host (e.g. Cloudinary), then paste the image URL into the admin dashboard for each slot. The website will use the images you set, or the built-in defaults if a slot is empty.
