Privacy Policy

Glens Residential Home (Glens Properties LLP) is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal data in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Glens Residential Home is operated by Glens Properties LLP (McKillop Family). Our registered manager is Siobhan McHugh. Our address is 63 Middlepark Road, Cushendall, Ballymena, BT44 0SQ. You can contact us by phone on 028 2177 1396 or via the contact form on this website.

What data we collect

When you use our contact form or get in touch with us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange visits, or provide information you have requested.

Legal basis and purpose

We process your data on the basis of your consent (when you submit the contact form) or our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries. We do not use your data for marketing unless you have given us clear consent to do so.

How long we keep your data

We keep contact form submissions and enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards for record-keeping. After that, we delete or anonymise the data unless we are required to keep it for legal or regulatory reasons.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data (where we have no lawful reason to keep it)
  • Object to processing or ask us to restrict processing
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on it
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details above.

Security

We take appropriate steps to keep your personal data secure and to prevent unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page. Last updated: March 2025.
